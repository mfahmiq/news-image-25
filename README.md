A newspaper front page summing up the world news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026** in one image.

## The stories depicted

1. **CIA & spy chiefs hacked** — CNN reported on 25 November that the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence were hit by a cyberattack affecting classified systems; the FBI's crime database was hit by a suspected breach in the same wave.
2. **Ebola medics flee fighting** — the WHO evacuated health workers from a hospital in Ituri, DR Congo, after they sheltered there for three days from fierce fighting, in the middle of the country's Ebola outbreak.
3. **NATO jets to Poland** — NATO planned to deploy early-warning aircraft to Poland after missiles landed in the country during an attack on Ukraine.
4. **Indonesia VP resigns** — Indonesia's Vice-President resigned amid political strain.
5. **Iowa blast today** — the US Army scheduled a controlled detonation of about 10,000 lb of unstable lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant on Wednesday morning, 25 November, after days of delays.

## How it works

`main.py` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`. The program runs with no network, as the rules require.
